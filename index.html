Family recipes
All four recipes below are naturally gluten-free and dairy-free adaptable — perfect for Heady and Rub. Each recipe serves the whole group (12–13 adults + kids). Scale down as needed.
Banana Oat Pancakes
25 min Stovetop Breakfast · Tue Jun 30 & Fri Jul 3
Serves 15 Naturally GF Naturally DF

Ingredients

Batter (per batch of ~12 pancakes)
4 ripe bananas, mashed
2 cups rolled oats (certified GF)
1 cup cottage cheese
4 large eggs
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp cinnamon
Pinch of salt
For cooking & topping
Coconut oil or butter for pan
Maple syrup
Fresh strawberries & blueberries
For the bacon (serves 15)
3 lbs bacon (about 4–5 packs)

Instructions

1Blend the batter: Add oats, cottage cheese, eggs, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt to a blender. Blend until smooth (about 30 seconds). Add mashed bananas and pulse a few times — some banana chunks are great.
2Rest the batter: Let sit 5 minutes — the oats absorb liquid and batter thickens up.
3Cook the bacon first: Lay bacon strips in a cold pan, cook on medium heat turning occasionally, until crispy. Drain on paper towels. Keep warm in a 200°F oven.
4Cook pancakes: Heat a non-stick pan or griddle over medium-low. Add a little coconut oil or butter. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook 2–3 min until bubbles form and edges look set, then flip. Cook 1–2 min more.
5Keep warm: Place finished pancakes on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven while you cook remaining batches.
6Serve: Stack pancakes, top with fresh berries and maple syrup. Serve with bacon alongside.
💡 Tips: The riper the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the pancakes — spotted bananas are perfect. Batter can be made the night before and refrigerated. For the patriotic Jul 3 version, top with strawberries, bananas & blueberries in red-white-blue stripes!
Surf & Turf — Garlic Butter Shrimp & Seared Steak
45 min Stovetop + Grill Dinner · Tue Jun 30
Serves 12–15 Naturally GF

Ingredients

Steak
4–5 lbs ribeye or NY strip steaks
2 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp kosher salt
2 tsp black pepper
2 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
4 tbsp butter (DF butter for Rub's portion)
4 garlic cloves, smashed
Fresh rosemary or thyme sprigs
Shrimp
3 lbs large shrimp, peeled & deveined
6 tbsp butter (or DF butter)
6 garlic cloves, minced
1 tsp red pepper flakes
Juice of 2 lemons
¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

1Season steaks: Pat steaks dry. Rub with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Let rest at room temp 30 min before cooking.
2Grill steaks: Preheat grill to high. Grill steaks 3–5 min per side depending on thickness and desired doneness. In the last 2 min, add butter, smashed garlic, and herb sprigs to a foil pan on the grill and baste steaks. Rest 5 min before slicing.
3Cook shrimp (stovetop): Melt butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add garlic and red pepper flakes — cook 1 min until fragrant. Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook 2 min per side until pink and curled. Remove from heat, squeeze lemon juice over, toss with parsley.
4Serve together: Slice steak against the grain. Plate with shrimp alongside, with mashed potatoes and roasted veggies (recipes below).
💡 Tips: For Rub, keep a small portion of shrimp aside cooked in DF butter. Don't overcook shrimp — they go from perfect to rubbery in 30 seconds! Steak should rest before cutting or the juices run out.
Creamy Mashed Potatoes & Seasoned Roasted Vegetables
50 min Oven + Stovetop Side dish · Pairs with Surf & Turf
Serves 12–15 Naturally GF

Ingredients

Mashed Potatoes
5 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes
8 tbsp butter (DF butter for Rub's portion)
1 cup warm heavy cream or milk (oat milk for Rub)
4 garlic cloves, roasted or minced
Salt & white pepper to taste
Fresh chives for topping
Roasted Vegetables
3 bell peppers, chunked
3 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
1 large red onion, wedged
2 cups cherry tomatoes
2 cups broccoli florets
4 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp Italian seasoning
½ tsp onion powder
Salt & pepper to taste
Fresh lemon juice to finish

Instructions

1Roast the veggies: Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss all vegetables with olive oil and all seasonings. Spread in a single layer across 2 large baking sheets — don't crowd or they'll steam instead of roast. Roast 25–30 min, flipping halfway, until edges are caramelized. Squeeze lemon juice over before serving.
2Boil potatoes: Peel and cube potatoes into 1.5-inch chunks. Place in a large pot, cover with cold salted water. Bring to a boil, then simmer 15–18 min until fork-tender.
3Mash: Drain potatoes, return to warm pot. Mash with a hand masher or ricer. Add warm butter and cream gradually, stirring until smooth. Season generously with salt and white pepper.
4Rub's DF portion: Set aside a small portion before adding regular butter/cream. Mash Rub's serving separately with DF butter and oat milk. Label it clearly!
5Serve: Top mashed potatoes with chives. Serve roasted veggies in a large bowl — the colors will be beautiful!
💡 Tips: Yukon Gold potatoes are creamier and less starchy than russets — worth the upgrade. Don't over-mix or potatoes turn gluey. The veggies can be prepped (chopped & seasoned) the night before and stored in bags.
Build-Your-Own Taco Salad Bar
30 min Stovetop Dinner · Wed Jul 1
Serves 12–15 Naturally GF DF adaptable

Ingredients

Seasoned Taco Meat
3 lbs ground beef (or half beef / half chicken)
2 tbsp chili powder
1 tbsp cumin
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp smoked paprika
½ tsp oregano
Salt & pepper to taste
½ cup water
Salad bar toppings
3 heads romaine, chopped
2 cans black beans, rinsed
2 cups corn (from cob or frozen)
2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
3 avocados, diced (or guacamole)
2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
1 cup sour cream
1 cup salsa
1 cup tortilla chips, crushed
Pickled jalapeños
Lime wedges & fresh cilantro

Instructions

1Cook the meat: Brown ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain excess fat. Add all spices and ½ cup water. Stir and simmer 5 min until sauce thickens and coats the meat. Taste and adjust seasoning.
2Set up the bar: Arrange all toppings in separate bowls down the table. Put cheese and sour cream at one end, guac and salsa in the middle, chips and hot stuff at the end.
3Build your bowl: Start with a bed of romaine, add taco meat, then pile on your favorites. Finish with crushed chips for crunch and a squeeze of lime.
4For Rub (DF): All toppings are naturally DF except cheese and sour cream — just skip those. Guacamole, salsa, beans, corn, and tomatoes are all safe and delicious!
5For the little kids: Serve meat and toppings separately — kids love choosing their own. Keep it mild and skip jalapeños for Oli, Rub, Will, and Wren.
💡 Tips: Make the taco seasoning double-batch and save half for taco night later. The meat can be cooked 2 hours ahead and kept warm in a covered pot. Warm the black beans in a small pot with a pinch of cumin and salt — so much better than cold from the can.
